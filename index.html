About Me

The Origin Hunter is run by Sarah Maywood, a family history researcher with over 15 years’ experience exploring genealogy and historical records.

I enjoy uncovering the people and stories behind the names, following records and clues to build a fuller picture of a family’s past. Going beyond names and dates to add depth and detail — putting some “meat on the bones” of your family story.

My approach is friendly, thorough and personal, with each family’s history researched with care, patience and attention to detail.
