About Me

The Origin Hunter is run by Sarah Maywood, a family history researcher with over 15 years’ experience exploring genealogy and historical records.

I don’t just build family trees. I put the “meat on the bones” and uncover the people behind the names.

Got a family mystery or hit a brick wall?
Deep Dive Research is ideal if there is one particular question you would like to explore in more detail. Perhaps an ancestor seems to disappear from the records, a family relationship is unclear, or you have reached a point where you are unsure how to continue. I’ll follow the available records and clues as far as they allow. Sometimes this leads to a clear answer; sometimes it helps narrow the possibilities or identify new avenues to investigate. As with all family-history research, some questions cannot be answered definitively from the surviving records.

Sometimes the records uncover a story worth hearing.

Record Availability & Research Coverage

Historical record availability varies by country, region and time period. Some records may be restricted, unavailable online or outside the sources I am able to access. Before research begins, I will check whether your project falls within the areas I am able to investigate effectively and let you know if there are any limitations.

Certificates & Additional Document Costs

Birth, marriage and death indexes can often be located through subscription research sites, but the full certificates themselves are not normally included with those subscriptions. Where a certificate is needed to confirm important details or progress the research, it may need to be purchased separately from the General Register Office or other relevant archive.

I will always discuss any additional document costs with you before ordering.

How It Works

1. Complete the Client Information Form here

If you have any questions about the Client Information Form before submitting it, please feel free to email me.

2. Choose your research package

3. Research begins once agreed

4. Receive your family tree and research summary

Your family tree will normally be researched and built on Ancestry.com, or developed from a GEDCOM file you provide. Where the tree is held on Ancestry.com, you can view the tree and the records and information I have added. Once the research is complete, I can give you guest access to the tree. The tree can also be exported as a GEDCOM file, which can be uploaded to many other genealogy platforms if you prefer to keep or continue your tree elsewhere.

Rather than producing a lengthy written report for every project, I provide a concise summary of the main findings, any important uncertainties, and suggested next steps where relevant.

If you ask me to work directly on your existing family tree and give me Editor access, I may make reasonable evidence-based additions, corrections or amendments as part of the research.
