Want to take your family tree much further?
Deep Dive Research gives me more time to follow several family lines, add further generations where the records allow, and look more closely at the people already in your tree. This can include finding additional ancestors, tracing where they lived and worked, adding photographs and stories where available, and using a wider range of records to build a fuller picture of your family history.

The extra research time also gives me scope to investigate any family mysteries, brick walls, unclear relationships or conflicting records that emerge along the way. I’ll follow the available evidence as far as it allows, with the aim of adding as much useful, well-supported information to your tree as possible. As with all family-history research, some lines may be easier to extend than others and not every question can be answered from the surviving records.

Research Promise

If I am building a new family tree for you:
If I am unable to add any new verified family-history information to the tree, I will refund the 50% upfront payment and no further balance will be due.

If I am working on an existing family tree:
The likelihood of finding new information will depend partly on how much research has already been carried out. More developed trees may offer fewer opportunities for new discoveries. Before research begins, I will review the information you provide and may decline a project if there appears to be too little scope for meaningful new findings.

If I do proceed with an existing tree and I am unable to add any new verified information beyond what you have already supplied, I will refund the 50% upfront payment and no further balance will be due.

The Research Promise does not apply to Additional Research charged at £25 per hour.

Record Availability & Research Coverage

Historical record availability varies by country, region and time period. Some records may be restricted, unavailable online or outside the sources I am able to access. Before research begins, I will check whether your project falls within the areas I am able to investigate effectively and let you know if there are any limitations.

Certificates & Additional Document Costs

Birth, marriage and death indexes can often be located through subscription research sites, but the full certificates themselves are not normally included with those subscriptions. Where a certificate is needed to confirm important details or progress the research, it may need to be purchased separately from the General Register Office or other relevant archive.

I will always discuss any additional document costs with you before ordering.

How It Works

1. Choose your research package

2. Complete the Client Information Form here If you have any questions about the Client Information Form before submitting it, please feel free to email me.

3. I will review the information you have provided and confirm whether I am able to take on the research.

4. If I am able to take on the research, I will confirm the next steps with you by email and send your invoice for the 50% upfront payment.

5. Research begins once the 50% upfront payment has been received. I aim to finish the agreed research within 28 days of receiving payment and having everything needed to begin. Some projects may be finished sooner, depending on the research package, the complexity of the work and the availability of records. If for any reason I am unable to finish the research within this timeframe, I will let you know as soon as possible.

6. When the agreed research is finished and ready for you to review, I will contact you and send the final 50% balance invoice. Once the balance has been paid, you can review the research and I will complete the final handover arrangements.

Your family tree will normally be researched and built within your own Ancestry account.

If you do not already have an Ancestry account, you will need to register for one before the research begins. Ancestry offers a free account, which is enough to create and own your family tree and to view the tree structure, names, relationships, timeline notes, stories, photographs and other information I add. Once the new tree has been created, I will need temporary Editor access for the duration of the research. Some historical records, documents and record images may require an Ancestry subscription before they can be viewed in full.

If you would prefer me to work directly on an existing Ancestry tree, I will require temporary Editor access to that tree. As part of the research, I may make reasonable evidence-based additions, corrections or amendments where appropriate.

For the best and most complete result, I recommend using Ancestry. If you do not wish to use Ancestry, I can provide your finished family tree as a GEDCOM file instead. A GEDCOM file transfers the core family-tree information, such as names, relationships, dates, places and notes, but it does not include the actual photographs, document images or other media attached to the tree, and some source information may not transfer in the same way.

For all fixed-price research packages, 50% of the package price is payable before research begins. The remaining 50% is payable when the agreed research has been finished and is ready for you to review.

When the research is complete, I will contact you to confirm that the project is finished. You will then be able to review the finished tree at your own pace, after which my Editor access can be removed.
